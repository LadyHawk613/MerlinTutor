About Me

Hello! I'm Christy Winstead, a passionate educator with over 25 years of experience helping students of all ages discover the joy of learning and making complex subjects accessible and engaging.

I believe that every student has the potential to excel when given the right guidance and support. My teaching philosophy centers on building confidence through understanding, using real-world examples, and adapting my methods to each student's unique learning style.

Whether you're struggling with basic concepts or looking to advance your skills, I'm here to help you achieve your educational goals in a supportive and encouraging environment.
